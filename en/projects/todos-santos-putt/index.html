Todos Santos Putt

Golf without the water. A mini golf course built by local artists, with a container bar. An idea for the town.

Swipe or use the arrows. The full presentation is available for download.

Cabo has championship golf. Todos Santos cannot have a golf course, and it should not: there is no water for it. But it can have something that does not exist in Todos Santos, La Paz or Los Cabos: a mini golf course designed and built by the town's artists, with a container bar, shaded for daytime play and built for evenings. For families, visitors and locals. This is the version of golf that fits here.

The references (Swingers in New York and London, Puttshack, PopStroke) show the format works as a night-out destination, not just a kids' game: the round sells the ticket and the bar makes the business. The Todos Santos version is smaller, more local and more ours.

The idea

  • Nine holes, nine artists. Each hole is designed and built by a Todos Santos artist around a local story: the mission, the sugar mill, Cerritos surf, the whales, the desert in bloom. In the permanent version, 18 holes on two levels.
  • A bar you come to on purpose. Two containers, a patio under shade sails, cocktails with local ingredients, snacks, gelato at the last hole.
  • Day and night. Families and visitors by day under shade; lit holes, leagues, tournaments, theme nights and birthdays at night. A small town runs on regulars.

Why here

There's a 400 m² lot next to Estudio 52, steps from downtown, with 32 meters of street frontage: a wedge that opens from 9 to 18 meters, so the lit course and the bar are the storefront. Todos Santos has first-rate restaurants and galleries but very few places to gather at night that serve everyone at once: expat residents, local families, surfers, hotel and rental guests, groups. A place like this pays nine local artists for permanent work, creates full-time jobs, and gives visitors one more reason to stay one more night.

Planning estimates at Baja prices; nothing is quoted yet. Everything that costs money in Phase 0 (containers, art, lights, bar equipment) carries over to the permanent version. Running the pop-up costs about $14,000 USD a month: four full-time people, land lease, power and maintenance.

Assumes about $14 USD a round and average bar spend of $16–18 per person. What would tell us yes: a full course December to March, about 45 rounds a day. Thirty rounds a day is a slow Tuesday at any of the references.

The holes

Nine artists, one hole each, $20–30k MXN per hole plus materials. If you want to build one, it's the most fun part.
