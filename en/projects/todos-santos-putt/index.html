Todos Santos Putt

Golf without the water. An artist-built 18-hole course and container bar in the heart of the pueblo mágico.

Swipe or use the arrows. The full deck is available for download.

Cabo has championship golf. Todos Santos cannot have a golf course, and it should not: there is no water for it. But it can have something that does not exist anywhere in Baja California Sur: an 18-hole mini golf course designed and built by the town's artists, with a container bar where people come at night for a drink, a round, and to stay a while.

The idea follows a proven model in other cities: places like Swingers in New York and London or PopStroke in the US, where the game sells the ticket and the bar makes the business. The Todos Santos version is smaller, more local, and more ours.

What it is

  • 18 holes, 9 artists. Every hole designed and built by a Todos Santos artist around one local story: the mission, the sugar mill, Cerritos surf, the whales, the desert bloom. Contoured putting turf with real breaks, so golfers come back.
  • A bar you come for. Two shipping containers, a patio under sails, 10 cocktails on local ingredients, snacks, gelato at the 18th. Order from the course by app.
  • Night mode. Lit holes, DJ Thursdays, league Tuesdays, monthly tournaments, buyouts for bachelor parties and retreats. Families and tourists by day under shade; the town's night out after dark.

Why it is good for the town

Todos Santos has first-rate restaurants and galleries, but very few places to gather at night that serve everyone at once: expat residents, local families, surfers, hotel and rental guests, groups. A venue like this creates decent jobs (5 full-time positions from Phase 0, at market wages with benefits), pays nine local artists for permanent work, and gives visitors one more reason to stay one more night.

The site

400 m² in town with 32 meters of street frontage. A wedge that widens from 9 to 18 meters, so the lit course and the bar are the storefront. Nine holes at grade in two columns; a 40-foot container bar at the wide end; and in the permanent phase, holes 10 to 18 on a deck above with the 18th on the bar roof.

The gate for Phase 1 is clear: 45 or more rounds a day and $14 USD or more in bar spend per player across December through March. Everything in Phase 0 that costs real money (containers, art, lights, bar kit) carries into the permanent build.

Break-even is 17 rounds a day: that covers every fixed cost of the pop-up (about $14,000 USD a month in wages, land lease, utilities, maintenance and marketing; the model uses $17,000 as a cushion). Figures are planning estimates in USD, not quotes.

Investor terms

  • Investors fund 100% of the $150,000 USD Phase 0.
  • The operating partner contributes site control (lease with a negotiated purchase option), the build and the operation, with no salary until the raise is repaid.
  • Quarterly distributions of all free cash: 100% to investors until capital is returned (1.0x), then 60 / 40 until 2.0x, then 50 / 50.
  • Phase 0 investors get first right to fund Phase 1 on the same terms, and first look at every future venue.
