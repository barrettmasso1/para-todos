Main Street: Urban Improvement Proposal

A simple, low-cost, high-impact intervention: native planting, warm lighting and organized parking, without losing a single space.

Todos Santos' main street is the town's first impression: for the neighbor going out for bread, for the visitor arriving from Cabo or La Paz, for the person who has lived here 40 years. Today it is a strip of dirt with overhead cables, no shade, no light at night, and cars parked wherever they fit. It works, but it does not represent us.

This proposal is not about paving or changing the character of the town. It is the opposite: preserve the essence of Todos Santos and, with simple interventions, make the street more beautiful, safer, better lit and more organized. Without losing parking capacity. At no cost to the municipality.

Phase 1: planting, lighting and order

Fast to implement and low cost. Everything can be done with local labor and regional materials.

Pedestrian walkway on the storefront side

  • Compacted fine gravel with a natural stone edge
  • Low-water native plants
  • Warm, discreet lighting (2700K LED)
  • Open access to every storefront

Planted islands and parking on the east side

  • Planted islands every 10 meters, each with 2 to 3 trees and desert plants
  • Natural rock borders, no built planters
  • Multiple entry and exit points to the parking
  • Native vegetation and shade trees: mesquite, palo verde, agave, yucca, cacti and grasses
  • Current parking capacity is kept and organized

Phase 2: organization and signage

Defined access and circulation, discreet signage, and consolidated tree cover. This phase requires coordination with the delegación and the municipality.

Phase 3: underground infrastructure

Underground power lines, paving of the parking area, and integration with the side streets. A medium-term project that becomes much easier to push once the street has already shown what it can be.

Why start here

Because it is visible, it is fast, and it depends on nobody but us. A street with trees, warm light and organized parking changes how the town feels at night, how long visitors stay, and what every storefront on that street is worth. It also proves something important: the community can solve its own problems when it organizes.

How you can help

  • Donate: each planted island has a defined cost in trees, plants, rocks and lights. Sponsor a whole one or give what you can.
  • Materials: nursery-grown native plants, rocks, gravel, warm-light LED fixtures.
  • Time: planting and installation days with neighbors.
  • Businesses on the street: adopt the stretch in front of your storefront and maintain it.

Images are concept renders to illustrate the proposal. The final design is set with the neighbors and the businesses on the street.
