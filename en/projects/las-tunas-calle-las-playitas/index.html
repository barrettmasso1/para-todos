Las Tunas: Light and Landscape on Calle Las Playitas

Landscape lighting, native planting and, in time, power lines underground. A street that already has small businesses and lacks the light and shade to walk it.

Calle Las Playitas, in the heart of Las Tunas, is turning into something nobody planned: a gelato shop, cafés, workshops, small stores, studios. Little businesses opening one after another on a street that is still dirt, overhead cables, zero lighting and zero shade. By day it works. By night it does not exist.

This project is not about paving or changing the character of Las Tunas. It is, as we say among neighbors, a bit of lipstick: warm light at ground level, desert trees and plants in natural rock islands, a gravel walkway in front of the storefronts. Enough that the street can be walked, looks good and feels safe. Without losing a single parking space. At no cost to the municipality.

Phase 1: light and landscape

Fast to implement and low cost. Everything can be done with local labor and regional materials.

Pedestrian walkway on the storefront side

  • Compacted fine gravel with a natural stone edge
  • Low-water native plants
  • Warm, discreet lighting (2700K LED) at ground level
  • Open access to every storefront

Planted islands and parking on the east side

  • Islands every 10 meters, each with 2 to 3 trees and desert plants
  • Natural rock borders, no built planters
  • Multiple entry and exit points to the parking
  • Shade trees and native vegetation: mesquite, palo verde, agave, yucca, cacti and grasses
  • Current parking capacity is kept and organized

Phase 2: order and signage

Defined access and circulation, discreet signage, and consolidated tree cover. This phase requires coordination with the delegación and the municipality.

Phase 3: power lines underground

Underground electrical lines, paving of the parking area, and integration with the side streets. A medium-term project that becomes much easier to push once the street has already shown what it can be.

Why start here

Because it is visible, it is fast, and it depends on nobody but us. A street with trees, warm light and organized parking changes how Las Tunas feels at night, how long people stay, and what every business on that street is worth. It also proves something important: a community can solve its own problems when it organizes.

How you can help

  • Donate: each island has a defined cost in trees, plants, rocks and lights. Sponsor a whole one or give what you can.
  • Materials: nursery-grown native plants, rocks, gravel, warm-light LED fixtures.
  • Time: planting and installation days with neighbors.
  • Businesses on the street: adopt the stretch in front of your storefront and maintain it.

Images are concept renders to illustrate the proposal. The final design is set with the neighbors and the businesses on the street.
